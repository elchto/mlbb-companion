# All 104 Mobile Legends items

> Every MLBB equipment item with its tier, shop category, gold cost, stat line and the text of every passive and active it carries. Grouped by category; an item that belongs to more than one appears under each. Source page: https://mlbbhub.com/items. Recipe tree and the heroes who build an item are on its own page at https://mlbbhub.com/items/{item-slug}.

## How to read these tables

Tier 3 is the finished equipment a build ends on and tier 2 the intermediate pieces, with one exception: the three potions are tier 3 but are consumables, and this site's build simulator files them with the hero special items that do not consume an equipment slot. Tier 1 covers two different things: the basic components that build into those, and the entries Liquipedia records no tier for at all, which this catalog files as tier 1 rather than guessing one. That second group is every Jungling and Roaming entry plus the hero-specific items, and those are not build components; an item's own page, linked from its row, shows what it builds into. A tier is the source's own label and the source is not always consistent with itself: where a tier 1 row has a recipe of its own it fits neither group, because the source tiers it one way in its data and another way in its prose. Read that item's page before treating its tier as settled.

Availability is a purchase restriction where the source records one, such as an item only a single hero can buy. Some are cut short at source and say what they gate without finishing the sentence, so treat a restriction as a reason to check the item's own page rather than as the full rule.

Cost is the gold price the source records for the item; a cost of 0 means no purchase price is recorded for that entry, not that it is free. A stat written with a percent sign is a percentage and everything else is a flat value. A unique attribute is a stat the source records outside the normal stat block, and this site's build simulator adds it into a build's totals like any other stat. Separately, some named unique passives do not stack: a second item carrying one adds no more of that effect. Only the repeated effect is wasted, not the item, which still contributes its stats and whatever else it does. A shared name is not proof of that on its own: this site records the confirmed groups itself and marks at least one shared name, Blessing, as unrelated effects that both apply. The per-item pages and the build simulator carry those groupings.

Inside a category the rows run from tier 3 down to tier 1 and alphabetically within each tier. That ordering carries no ranking. Nothing here is a performance measurement either: this site measures win, pick and ban rates per HERO and never per item, so for what to build, read a hero's own page and its reviewed build rather than inferring anything from this catalog.
